## PROFILE

Plasma physicist with **10+ years of international experience** in **numerical and theoretical modelling of magnetized plasmas**, from laboratory HEDP to **astrophysical relativistic flows and compact-object environments**. My research connects plasma instabilities, particle acceleration, magnetic-field generation, and shock physics — themes directly aligned with CmPA's solar-wind, magnetospheric, and compact-object programmes. I combine large-scale **kinetic (PIC), hybrid, and MHD** simulations with the design of laboratory experiments at world-leading laser facilities, allowing predictive plasma models to be benchmarked against controlled measurements. I have a proven record of **competitive funding (MSCA 99/100, top 1.62 %)**, high-impact publications (*Nature Physics*, *PRL*, *ApJ*), international leadership of beamtime campaigns, and **Master's-level teaching** at Sorbonne Université. I am ready to lead an independent group at the interface of mathematical plasma astrophysics, kinetic plasma theory, and laboratory benchmarking.
